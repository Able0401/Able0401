# Hyun Seung Moon

**I study how people learn what can't be taught — by talking with AI that plays the other side.**

Ph.D. student at [AI Experience Lab](https://ai-experience-lab.github.io/), KAIST Industrial Design, advised by Prof. Tak Yeon Lee. I build **AI interlocutors** — simulated patients, historical figures, customers, negotiation counterparts — and study how conversational simulation with them cultivates social skills, tacit knowledge, and judgment.

### Selected work

- 🏛️ **[HistoChat](https://github.com/Able0401/Histochat)** — LLM historical personas for middle-school history education. *CSCW 2025 full paper (co-first author)* · [demo](https://histochat-bbf8e.web.app/)
- 🩺 **Flexible psychiatric AI interviewer** — clinician-supervised LLM history-taking, evaluated with 1,440 simulated dialogues + 19 clinicians. *CHI 2026 full paper* · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790970)
- 🎙️ **Osler** — voice AI agent for breast cancer preoperative counseling, in a prospective pilot clinical study at Asan Medical Center
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Synthetic customer agents** — grounded in 4,000-respondent survey data, deployed company-wide at LG Electronics (87% validated accuracy)

I care about research that survives contact with the real world — every system I study is built to be deployed in classrooms, hospitals, and enterprises.
