# Hyun Seung Moon

Ph.D. student in Industrial Design at KAIST, in the [AI Experience Lab](https://ai-experience-lab.github.io/) with Prof. Tak Yeon Lee. Minor in Data Science.

I build AI that plays the other side of a conversation, such as a patient, a historical figure, or a negotiation counterpart. Then I study whether talking with it helps people learn skills that are hard to teach directly.

**Looking for a Summer 2027 research internship in the US.**

## Papers

- **HistoChat**: historical personas for middle-school history classes, used in class for six weeks. CSCW 2025, co-first author. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757534) · [Code](https://github.com/Able0401/Histochat) · [Demo](https://histochat-bbf8e.web.app/)
- **Psychiatric history-taking with LLMs**: an AI interviewer tested in 1,440 simulated dialogues and reviewed by 19 clinicians. CHI 2026. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790970)
- **CineTalk**: a chatbot for seeing other viewpoints on a film. CUI 2026. [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3816046.3816281)
- **Creators and chat agents**: how short-form video creators work with conversational agents. CHI 2026 Extended Abstracts. [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772363.3798927)

## Ongoing projects

- **Osler**: voice agent for breast-cancer surgery counseling, in a pilot study at Asan Medical Center
- **Voice virtual patient**: simulated patients for psychiatric interview practice, with Severance Hospital
- **Synthetic consumer agents**: built from a 4,000-person survey and used by 47 staff at LG Electronics for four months
- **Artificial Social Actor**: hand a negotiation to an agent you set up yourself. Master's thesis. [Code](https://github.com/Able0401/artificial-social-actor-demo) · [Demo](https://able0401.github.io/artificial-social-actor-demo/)
