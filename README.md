# Hyun Seung Moon

**I study how people learn what can't be taught — by talking with AI that plays the other side.**

Ph.D. student at [AI Experience Lab](https://ai-experience-lab.github.io/), KAIST Industrial Design (minor in Data Science), advised by Prof. Tak Yeon Lee. I build **AI interlocutors** — simulated patients, historical figures, customers, negotiation counterparts — and study how conversational simulation with them cultivates social skill, tacit knowledge, and judgment. Every system below was deployed with real users: classrooms, hospitals, or an enterprise.

📌 **Seeking a Summer 2027 research internship in the US** (human-AI interaction · LLM agents · evaluation · AI for education and health).

### Selected work

- 🏛️ **[HistoChat](https://github.com/Able0401/Histochat)** — LLM historical personas for middle-school history classes, deployed for six weeks. *CSCW 2025 full paper (co-first author)* · [demo](https://histochat-bbf8e.web.app/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757534)
- 🩺 **Flexible psychiatric AI interviewer** — clinician-supervised LLM history-taking, evaluated with 1,440 simulated dialogues and 19 clinicians. *CHI 2026 full paper* · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790970)
- 🤝 **[Artificial Social Actor](https://github.com/Able0401/artificial-social-actor-demo)** — delegate a negotiation to an LLM agent you configure (position, interest, disclosure and inference strategy). Master's thesis prototype · [live demo](https://able0401.github.io/artificial-social-actor-demo/)
- 🎙️ **Osler** — voice AI agent for breast-cancer preoperative counseling, in a prospective pilot study at Asan Medical Center
- 🧠 **Voice virtual patient** — LLM simulated patients for psychiatric interview training with Severance Hospital
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Synthetic consumer agents (SCAS)** — eight agent clusters grounded in a 4,000-respondent survey, used for four months by 47 practitioners across six departments at LG Electronics
- 💬 **CineTalk** — LLM chatbot for exploring diverse perspectives on film. *CUI 2026* · **Creators × conversational agents** in short-form video production. *CHI EA 2026*

### How I work

Build the system → deploy it where it is meant to be used → evaluate with mixed methods (large-scale simulated dialogues + interviews and field observation). Python · TypeScript/React · realtime voice LLM pipelines · multi-agent simulation.
